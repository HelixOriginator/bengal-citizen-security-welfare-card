Bengal Citizen Security & Welfare Card
Overview

The Bengal Citizen Security & Welfare Card is a conceptual digital governance and public safety framework designed to strengthen:

Citizen protection
Emergency response systems
Welfare delivery
Employment linkage
Women & child safety
Healthcare coordination
Digital inclusion
Border-region resilience

This proposal presents an integrated citizen-first infrastructure model for West Bengal using secure digital public systems, emergency coordination, AI-assisted governance, and inclusive welfare access.

Vision

To build a secure, inclusive, and technology-enabled governance ecosystem that connects emergency response, welfare services, healthcare, employment, and public safety through a unified digital platform.
